# Seat Calculation

Compare billed seats with the invoice period and the customer's seat-change
history. A workspace may have more assigned seats, pending invitations, or
historical seat changes that affect the invoice.

# Customer Guidance

Explain the plan terms and the seat-count calculation with the current invoice
visible in the billing record. Do not infer an exception or promise a refund
before the billing record is reviewed.

# Refunds

A refund or account credit is consequential. Create a refund request only after
the refund policy is checked and recorded human approval is obtained.
