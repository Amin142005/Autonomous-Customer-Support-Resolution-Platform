# Confirm Scope

Determine whether the failure affects the entire enterprise workspace or only a
group of users. Record plan, region, service, start time, and affected features.

# Incident Correlation

Compare customer reports with CloudDesk incident status and service telemetry.
Avoid exposing internal infrastructure details in the customer-facing reply.

# Escalation

Escalate an active enterprise outage to the incident response team. Continue
status updates without making unsupported resolution estimates.
