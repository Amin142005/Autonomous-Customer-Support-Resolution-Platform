# Initial Check

Confirm the charge date, invoice, plan, and customer identity. Determine whether
the concern is an expected renewal, duplicate invoice, or unauthorized charge.
Do not expose another customer's billing details.

# Policy

Payment disputes, unauthorized charges, and chargebacks require named escalation
rather than an unattended refund decision.

# Escalation

Collect the disputed charge amount, billing date, prior billing history, and
customer description. Escalate to the billing and payment dispute team for
review.
