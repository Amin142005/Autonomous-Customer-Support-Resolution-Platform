# Confirm Usage

Check the workspace storage usage and subscription limit. Distinguish a plan
quota from a temporary upload or service issue.

# Reduce Usage

Identify large or duplicated files without deleting customer data. Explain
available cleanup steps and plan options.

# Account Changes

Do not change a subscription or erase files without authorization. A consequential
storage or account change requires the documented approval process.
