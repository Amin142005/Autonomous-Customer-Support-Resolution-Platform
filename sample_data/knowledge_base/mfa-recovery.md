# Scope

Confirm whether the user is locked out after repeated MFA attempts or has lost
their authenticator device. Do not ask customers to provide passwords, MFA codes,
or recovery secrets in a support ticket.

# Checks

Verify the user and workspace through support-approved account checks. Review
MFA attempts and workspace security settings. If the account belongs to an
enterprise workspace, follow the enterprise recovery process.

# Resolution

Direct the customer to the approved recovery flow. Reset MFA only through a
documented account action that has the required authorization and audit record.
