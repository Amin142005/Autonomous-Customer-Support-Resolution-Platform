# Check Settings

Confirm the workspace sharing policy and the folder owner's permissions. External
sharing may be disabled by workspace policy or administrator settings.

# Review Collaborator Access

Verify that the external collaborator is invited through the approved sharing
flow. Distinguish a link-policy failure from a general workspace permission issue.

# Resolution

Explain the setting that controls sharing and ask an administrator to change it
when appropriate. Do not change workspace settings without authorization.
