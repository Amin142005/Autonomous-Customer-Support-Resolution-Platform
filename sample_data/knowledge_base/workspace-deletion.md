# Verify Request

Confirm the exact workspace, customer, authority to delete, and required retention
or legal review. Never interpret a ticket alone as approval to erase data.

# Policy

Account deletion and data erasure can require legal, retention, and billing
review. These actions must not run unattended.

# Escalation

Escalate to the account deletion team and record the requested scope, customer
identity, and review findings.
