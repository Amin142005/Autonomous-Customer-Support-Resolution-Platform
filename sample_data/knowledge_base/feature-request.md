---

# Capture Details

Record the customer use case, affected workspace, desired behavior, and any
deadline or business impact. Do not promise a release date.

# Assessment

Check whether existing settings provide a workaround. Categorize the request as
a low-severity product feedback case unless it has a material outage or policy
impact.

# Response

Explain that the request is recorded for product review and offer any available
workaround. Use a ticket summary rather than hidden internal planning details.
