---

# Preserve Evidence

Record the reported locations, devices, time range, and affected account actions.
Do not ask for passwords or MFA secrets.

# Immediate Protection

Guide account-security measures that do not destroy evidence, such as reviewing
active sessions and approved password or MFA recovery. Confirm workspace scope.

# Escalation

A suspected breach or compromise requires escalation to the security team. Include
scope and timestamps; do not make claims before evidence is reviewed.
