---

# Initial Actions

Stop destructive cleanup. Record when files disappeared, the affected folder,
the known customer, and whether workspace activity reports show moves or deletes.

# Investigation

Check trash, recent activity, and known workspace synchronization changes.
Avoid downloading or exporting unrelated customer data.

# Escalation

Suspected data loss is a policy escalation. Route the case to the data recovery
team with timestamps and scope; do not promise recovery outcomes.
